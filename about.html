Our Story

A decade of expertise, turned into a vision

After a decade managing events for top-tier clients at a leading agency, Sagar Gori set out to build something of his own. In 2023 the idea of Kre8tive Minds was born, and in June 2024 Kre8tive Minds Productions LLP officially launched.

From brand activations to grand celebrations, our innovative approach and commitment to exceptional service have quickly made us a trusted corporate event partner — bringing your vision to life with precision and care.

Our Philosophy

Great events aren't planned. They're felt.

Anyone can fill a room and follow a checklist. We design for emotion — the gasp at the reveal, the goosebumps during a speech, the moment a guest decides they'll never forget tonight.

That's why we begin every project with one question: how do you want people to feel when they leave? Everything — the lighting, the timing, the surprises — is reverse-engineered from that answer. It's what makes a Kre8tive Minds event unmistakably ours.

Meet the founders

Sagar Gori

Founder & CEO

Sagar Gori is the Founder of Kre8tive Minds Productions LLP, with over 10 years of experience in corporate events, BTL activations, and brand promotions across India. Based in Mumbai, he has successfully delivered impactful events for leading brands including Asian Paints, Aditya Birla, Kotak, IQEQ, Qiagen, and Rare India. A qualified event professional since earning his Diploma in Event Management in 2013, Sagar brought that decade of experience together in 2023 to build Kre8tive Minds — officially launched as an LLP in June 2024 — with a vision to create innovative, seamless and memorable event experiences. Today, he continues to lead the company with creativity, professionalism, and a commitment to exceeding client expectations.

Meghna Gori

Co-Founder

Meghna Gori, co-founder of Kre8tive Minds, brings 12 years of industry experience and expertise as a travel agent. Her deep understanding of the travel industry complements the event management side of the business, making her an essential part of the team. Meghna’s logistical acumen and ability to navigate the complexities of travel and event planning ensure seamless execution, whether the event is local or international.
